# Weekly call book

Feature: **customers → call_book** (`Weekly call book`).

Staff open **Customers → Call centre**. The week is Monday–Friday in Africa/Nairobi. Saturday and Sunday show who was not called and do not accept a new mark. “Called this week” comes from the call’s Monday date, so the list starts again on Monday without anyone clearing a box.

Every signed-in role except Field Sales receives `customers.call_book`. Field agents meet customers in person, so they do not work the phone list. Everyone else copies the number onto their own cell phone and records the result here. The screen does not place the call. Opening a customer or copying the number **claims that shop for the rest of the Nairobi day** so only one person can call them that day. A second person gets a conflict if they try to claim or mark the same customer after someone else already called them today. **Who called** shows every recorded call for a chosen calendar day and the staff who made those calls. Queue rows also show who called this week and last week, and flag when the callers changed.

**Updating notes:** On web and mobile, the same person who recorded a call this week can open that customer again (Weekly schedule → **Update**, or search) and save corrected notes or outcome. `POST /api/sales/call-book/{id}/mark/` updates their existing week row (`updated: true`) instead of inserting a duplicate. Another staff member who already called the customer today still receives 409.

Shops **not called last week** sit above other pending shops (after due call-later rows). Manager and Super Admin edit which wards fall on which day. Where no plan row is saved, every shop in one sub-county shares a call day. Sub-counties are handed out Monday through Friday in name order, then the list starts at Monday again. Shops with no county and no sub-county are spread across the week.

Called, call later, no answer, and unreachable all count for the week. Once a result is recorded, that customer leaves the working list until next Monday, or until a call-later time is reached. The list is paged, ten customers at a time, for the selected day. Each day's header names the sub-counties on that day. A blank sub-county is shown as No sub-county. The same sub-county is named once. Call later also records the time to call again (`HH:MM`, Nairobi). On the next load or refresh, a customer whose time has been reached is placed at the top of the list, earliest time first. A time that is still ahead stays in the normal order. The note stays so next Monday’s caller can see it. An optional follow-up date can be today or up to 90 days ahead. A note does not become an order. Customers with fewer than 9 phone digits are counted as cannot-call and stay off the list.

The Full retail preset turns the feature on. The phone uses the same list: ten customers at a time for the selected day, and a recorded call leaves that list. Field Sales do not see the call centre. A store can switch it off under Module Settings; the API then returns 403 and the nav item hides.
